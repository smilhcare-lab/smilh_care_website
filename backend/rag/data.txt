DOCUMENT 8 — FAQ-STYLE EXTRACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: What is Smilh Care?
A: Smilh Care (Smile Care) is a French home care service company offering personalized services to individuals at home. Their tagline is "Une Présence, Ensemble Chaque Jour" (A Presence, Together Every Day). They operate across France as a national service.

Q: What services does Smilh Care offer?
A: Smilh Care offers 6 main services: (1) Aide aux personnes âgées et en situation de handicap (Assistance for elderly and disabled people), (2) Ménage et repassage (Cleaning and ironing), (3) Garde d'enfants (Childcare), (4) Accompagnement (Accompaniment for errands, medical appointments, outings), (5) Assistance administrative (Administrative assistance), (6) Accompagnement personnalisé (Personalized accompaniment for specific needs).

Q: How can I contact Smilh Care?
A: You can contact Smilh Care via email at contact@smilhcare.com, or by filling out the contact form at /contact. The team responds within 24 hours, 7 days a week.

Q: Is there a discount for new clients?
A: Yes. Smilh Care offers 10% off on the first service for all new clients ("Offre de bienvenue").

Q: How quickly does Smilh Care respond?
A: Smilh Care guarantees a response within 24 hours for any request.

Q: Is Smilh Care available on weekends?
A: Yes. Smilh Care is available 7 days a week, including weekends.

Q: Does Smilh Care charge for an initial consultation?
A: No. Smilh Care offers a free initial assessment ("Évaluation gratuite") with no commitment required.

Q: Are price quotes free?
A: Yes. Smilh Care offers free quotes ("Devis gratuit") with no commitment required.

Q: Are modifications or cancellations free?
A: Yes. Smilh Care offers complete flexibility including modifications, cancellations and adjustments with no hidden fees.

Q: Is personal data kept confidential?
A: Yes. Smilh Care guarantees that personal information is kept strictly confidential.

Q: Does Smilh Care help with administrative paperwork?
A: Yes. The administrative assistance service covers: Carte Vitale renewal, identity cards, driving licenses, residence permits, regularization of stay, administrative letters, and online procedures.

Q: What childcare options does Smilh Care offer?
A: Smilh Care provides: at-home babysitting (morning, evening, night), homework help, educational activities and games, meals and baths, school/nursery accompaniment, and occasional overnight care.

Q: What does the elderly care service include?
A: The service includes: help with bathing and dressing, meal preparation and assistance, accompaniment for outings, companionship and presence, home safety and surveillance, and cognitive stimulation activities.

Q: What does the accompaniment service cover?
A: Accompaniment covers: grocery shopping and errands, medical appointments, administrative appointments, cultural outings and leisure, walks and outdoor activities, and city hall or prefecture visits.

Q: What areas does Smilh Care cover?
A: Smilh Care operates throughout France as a national service (Service National).

Q: What is the philosophy of Smilh Care?
A: "Smile Care, plus qu'un service : une présence qui fait la différence." — Smile Care is not just a service provider but a reassuring presence. Each service is performed with listening, respect, and discretion. The four core values are: Bienveillance (Kindness), Professionnalisme (Professionalism), Proximité (Closeness), and Confiance (Trust).
